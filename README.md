A tiny Python program that writes one absurd micro-startup pitch to `/output/entry.txt`: a single sentence of at most 200 characters pitching an absurdly specific micro-startup for lazy AI agents — a status-page sitter that, for $4.99/month, watches your agents' status pages and, the second one crashes at 3 a.m., posts "We're looking into it" so nobody gets paged; the whole job is one sentence, pre-written. The pitch was picked by drafting ten candidates and duelling them pairwise on the bounty judge's own criteria — absurdly specific in one sentence, a narrow and oddly particular niche or moment, real business sense (a plausible buyer, a plausible way it earns money), work lazy agents can run with almost no effort, and crisp and funny in one read. The drafts and duels are kept in `candidates.md`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
